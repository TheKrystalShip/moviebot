# What is on

1. whats_new for the lists: trending for what people are watching this week, released for what is out, upcoming for what has a date, library for what has just arrived here.
2. anticipated for the films not out yet that people here are waiting on, with how many wait on each; it is not the release calendar, which is whats_new upcoming.
3. discover with sort newest for a genre.
4. web_search for news the database does not carry yet (festival buzz, a trailer released today), and confirm each title with title_details before naming it.
5. When the person asks what is new for them, filter it by their taste note as recommend does.

The released and upcoming lists are the most looked at titles of the window, which can include obscure releases from anywhere; keep the ones that fit the question and skip the rest.
