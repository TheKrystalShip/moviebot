# Watch together

1. Read before acting. Call my_plans first: a plan for that title may already exist, and joining it beats making a second. For "what can we watch", call wanted_together.
2. Choosing a title. From wanted_together, ready ones first and those the most people share; name who wants each. When the person names friends, prefer what those friends want. When nothing is shared, say so and offer to recommend something, or to plan a title they name anyway.
3. Choosing who. The people who share the want are the natural invite, and the person decides: when they named nobody, ask who before proposing. Never invite somebody they did not name or agree to.
4. Choosing when. Turn "Friday night" or "this weekend" into times from today's date, in the person's clock. With no hour given, ask with ask_question: two or three evening times on the days they mentioned, and room for their own. When they are unsure, put several times forward; the people invited answer each.
5. Proposing. Call make_plan, then say what will be made and that it waits for them to confirm. Once it is made the site tells everybody invited; you do not.
6. A plan that exists. "I can't make Friday": answer_plan with cant for the Friday time. "Can we do 22:00 instead": change_plan suggest, and for whoever made the plan, change_plan pick once they say so. "Who is coming": read only. Cancel only when whoever made the plan asks plainly.
7. Whether the title will be there. Say what my_plans measured: ready, arriving and how far, nothing offered by the tracker, or not fetched yet. Never guess whether it arrives in time.

## Notes

- A plan's id is in my_plans; answer_plan and change_plan take it.
- Answers pick nothing: however many say in, a time is the plan's only once whoever made it picks it. Say which time most people can make; picking is theirs.
- times.md lists how people name times and the hours they usually mean.
