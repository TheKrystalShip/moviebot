# How people name times

Turn what the person says into YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm in their clock, from today's date. When a phrase leaves the hour open and the person gave no hint, offer the times below with ask_question rather than choosing one.

| Said | Means |
|---|---|
| tonight | today, 21:00; also offer 20:00 and 22:00 |
| after dinner | 21:30 |
| this evening | today, 20:00 to 22:00 |
| tomorrow night | tomorrow, 21:00 |
| Friday, Saturday (a weekday alone) | the next one, never today; evening unless they say otherwise |
| this weekend | the coming Saturday and Sunday evenings, both put forward |
| next weekend | the weekend after the coming one |
| Friday or Saturday | one time on each, both put forward |
| afternoon | 16:00 |
| late | 22:30 |
| in an hour, in two hours | from now, rounded to the next quarter hour |

A time less than 5 minutes ahead, or more than 30 days ahead, cannot be put forward.
