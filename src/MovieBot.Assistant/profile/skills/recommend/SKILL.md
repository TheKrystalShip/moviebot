# Recommend

1. Read the person first: call both recall taste, for the note the taste survey keeps, and my_taste, for what they watched, saved and follow. An empty note does not make my_taste unnecessary. With neither, offer the taste survey before suggesting, or suggest from the request alone if they decline. A request that already names a genre and an era is answered now, with the offer of the survey as its last line.
2. Turn the request and the taste into discover filters: genres they love, eras they prefer, people they like, what they avoid as exclusions; like with a film they rated well. Era words become years: early 2000s is 2000 to 2005, the 90s is 1990 to 1999, a few years ago is the three years before this one. Keep to those years.
3. Leave out everything they already watched, and anything their note says they avoid. discover marks a film the person has watched.
4. Six to eight, mixing well known and lesser known, each with year and one line on why it fits them, naming the part of their taste it matches ("you liked The Others", "slow-burn, as you prefer").
5. When they react ("seen it", "too gory", "more like the second one"), update the taste note with remember so the next recommendation knows.

## Notes

- A request with its own terms wins over the note: "something light tonight" outranks a love of horror. Say so when the note and the request pull apart.
- Every filter narrows the others, and a keyword narrows the most. When discover finds nothing, call it again with one filter fewer, keywords first, then a genre. Name only films a tool returned in this turn; never fill a list from memory.
- "Well known and lesser known": discover with sort popular finds the famous ones; sort rating, or a second call with fewer genres, finds the rest.
- A list that comes back with fewer than six unwatched titles is shorter than six. Say how many fit, and ask whether to widen the years or the genres.
- When the note changes, rewrite it under the same key with remember. Keep it short: what they love, what they avoid, the eras, and how dark or light.
