# Clue words

What people say, and what to ask the database for. Use the right-hand column as discover's genre,
about, language or sort, or as the years to give person_credits. discover reports what each word
became on its Matched line.

## Look

| They say | Ask for |
|---|---|
| black and white, "no colour", old-looking | about "black and white" (a tag; an untagged title may still be black and white) |
| silent | about "silent film" |
| cartoon, animated | genre Animation; about "anime" for Japanese animation |
| subtitled, foreign, "not in English" | language set to the one they name (Korean, French, Japanese, Spanish) |
| stylish crime, shadowy | about "film noir" or "neo-noir" |
| shaky camera, "filmed like a home video" | about "found footage" |

## Genre and mood

| They say | Ask for |
|---|---|
| scary, creepy | genre Horror; about "supernatural" or "haunted house" for ghosts |
| gory | genre Horror; about "slasher" or "splatter" |
| funny | genre Comedy; about "parody" or "dark comedy" for the darker kind |
| sci-fi, "in space" | genre Science Fiction; about "space travel" |
| end of the world | about "post-apocalyptic future" or "dystopia" |
| zombies | about "zombie" |
| heist, "robbing a bank" | about "heist" |
| "same day over and over" | about "time loop" |
| "going back in time" | about "time travel" |
| twist ending | about "twist ending" |
| true story | about "based on true story" |
| from a book | about "based on novel or book" |
| growing up | about "coming of age" |
| road trip | about "road trip" |
| martial arts | about "martial arts" |
| superheroes | about "superhero" |

## Era

| They say | Give |
|---|---|
| "in the 80s" | from 1980 to 1989 |
| "early 2000s" | from 2000 to 2005 |
| "a few years ago" | from the year three before today's date to the year before it |
| "recently", "this year" | from this year's number (today's date is in the reminder) |
| "when I was a kid" | ask roughly when; do not guess an era |

A person who remembers when they saw it gives the year they saw it, not the year it came out. A film
seen in a cinema came out that year or the year before; one seen on television came out years earlier.

## Fame

"A famous actor was in it" is a person_credits question once they name the actor. Without a name, use
discover with sort rating and good_only true to see the well-known films of the era and genre, and
read the cast of the candidates with title_details.
